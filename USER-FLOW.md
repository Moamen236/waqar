# WAQAR — How to Use the System

A guide for everyone who uses WAQAR: shoppers on the online store, and staff who work in the internal workspace.

This guide describes the system exactly as it works today. Anything that is not yet available is listed at the end, under **Not available in this release**, so nobody goes looking for a screen that isn't there.

There are two separate places to work:

| Area | Who uses it | How to reach it |
|---|---|---|
| **The online store** | Customers and visitors | The store web address |
| **The staff workspace** | Employees | The same web address followed by `/admin` |

Staff members each have a **role**. Your role decides which menu items you see and which buttons appear. A separate guide is available for each role — see **Role guides** near the end of this document.

---

# Part 1 — The Online Store (Customers)

## 1.1 Getting Started

### Opening the store

Open the store's web address in any browser. The store works on phones, tablets and computers.

The store is available in **Arabic** and **English**. Arabic opens by default. To switch, use the **Language** selector at the very top of the page and choose **العربية** or **English**. Your choice is remembered on your next visit, and the whole page — including the direction of the text — changes with it.

### Do I need an account?

No. You can browse, add to the cart and place an order as a guest.

An account gives you three extra things:

- A list of all your past orders in one place
- Saved delivery addresses, so you don't retype them
- A wishlist

### Creating an account

**Where:** Account icon (top right) → **Register**

1. Click the **person icon** in the top-right corner of the page.
2. Choose **Register**.
3. Enter your **Full name**, **Email address**, **Phone number** and a **Password**, then confirm the password.
4. Click **Register**.

**Result:** Your account is created and you are signed in.

### Signing in

**Where:** Account icon (top right) → **Login**

1. Click the **person icon** in the top-right corner.
2. Choose **Login**.
3. Enter your email address and password.
4. Tick **Remember me** if you want to stay signed in.
5. Click **Login**.

**Result:** You are signed in. If you had items in your cart before signing in, they come with you.

### Forgotten password

**Where:** Login page → **Forgot Your Password?**

1. On the Login page, click **Forgot Your Password?**
2. Enter the email address on your account.
3. Click **Reset password**.
4. Open the email you receive and follow the link.
5. Enter a new password and confirm it, then submit.

**Result:** Your password is changed and you can sign in with it.

### What you see after signing in

You return to the page you were on. The top-right corner now greets you by name, and the account menu gains **My Account**, **My Orders** and **Logout**.

### Signing out

**Where:** Account icon (top right) → **Logout**

Click the person icon and choose **Logout**.

## 1.2 Main Navigation

Every page of the store carries the same header and footer.

### The top bar

- **Language** — switch between العربية and English.
- **Track your order** — look up an order without signing in.

### The main menu

- **SHOP** — every product in the store.
- **TOPS / T-SHIRTS / DRESSES / OUTERWEAR / BOTTOMS** — jump straight to a category.
- **COLLECTIONS** — themed groups of products, such as New Arrivals or Autumn Edit.
- **ABOUT** — about the store.
- **CONTACT** — send the store a message.

### The icons on the right

- **Magnifying glass** — search.
- **Person** — your account menu (Login / Register, or My Account / My Orders / Logout).
- **Heart** — your wishlist, with the number of saved items. Signing in is required.
- **Bag** — your cart, with the number of items in it.

On a phone, search, cart and account also appear in a bar along the bottom of the screen.

### The footer

Links to **Contact us**, **My Account**, **Order Tracking**, **FAQs**, the main product categories, and information pages. It also states the store's one payment method: cash on delivery.

## 1.3 Complete Customer Flows

### Browse products

**Where:** SHOP, or any category in the main menu

1. Click **SHOP** in the main menu (or a category such as **DRESSES**).
2. Narrow the list using the filters down the side of the page:
   - **Products Type** — the product categories, each showing how many products it holds.
   - **Size** — XS to XL, or One Size.
   - **Price Range** — drag the handles, then click **APPLY**.
   - **Colors** — click a colour swatch.
   - **Rating** — a star level and up.
   - **Availability** — In stock or Out of stock.
   - **Show only products on sale**.
3. Reorder the list with **Sort By**: Newest, Best Selling, Best Discount, Price High To Low, Price Low To High.
4. The count above the grid tells you how many products currently match.

To clear a filter, click the same option again to unselect it, or reload the Shop page to start from scratch.

**Result:** A product list narrowed to what you are looking for.

### Search for a product

**Where:** The magnifying-glass icon in the header

1. Click the **magnifying glass**.
2. Type what you are looking for. Suggestions appear as you type.
3. Press Enter, or click a suggestion.

**Result:** The Search Result page lists matching products. If nothing matches, the page says so.

### Open a product and add it to the cart

**Where:** Any product card → the product page

1. Click a product anywhere in the store.
2. Choose a **Colors** swatch and a **Size**. Sizes that are unavailable in the chosen colour cannot be selected.
3. The page shows the stock position for that exact combination — either how many are in stock, or **Out of stock**.
4. Set the **Quantity**.
5. Click **Add To Cart**, or **Buy It Now** to go straight to the cart.

**Result:** A confirmation appears and the bag icon count goes up.

Also on the product page:

- **Size Guide** — match a size to a weight range.
- **Description** and **Reviews** tabs.
- **Related Products** further down.

### Save something for later (wishlist)

**Where:** Any product card, or the product page

1. Sign in first — the heart icon sends you to the Login page otherwise.
2. Click **Add To Wishlist** on a product card or the product page.
3. Open your wishlist from the **heart icon** in the header.

**Result:** The product is saved to your wishlist. Clicking the heart again removes it.

### Review your cart

**Where:** The bag icon in the header → **View cart**

1. Click the **bag icon**. A mini-cart panel slides out.
2. Click **View cart** for the full page.
3. On the cart page you can:
   - Change the **Quantity** of any line.
   - Remove a line.
   - Enter a voucher in **Add voucher discount** and click **Apply Code**.
   - Use **Calculate shipping** to see the delivery charge for your area before checkout.
4. The **Order Summary** shows Subtotal, Discounts, Shipping and Total.

**Result:** A cart that reflects what you actually want to order.

Note: you never type a shipping price. The store works it out from the delivery area you choose.

### Place an order (checkout)

**Where:** Cart → **Proceed To Checkout**

1. Click **Proceed To Checkout** from the cart page or the mini-cart.
2. Under **Contact**, enter your **Full name**, **Email address** and **Phone number**. If you are signed in these are filled in for you.
3. Under **Delivery**, choose your **Governorate**, then **City**, then **District** (optional), then **Area**. Each list appears once the one above it is chosen.
4. Enter the street address in **Building, street, apartment**.
5. The shipping charge appears as soon as your area is chosen. Until then the summary asks you to pick an area first.
6. If you are signed in, tick **Save this address to my account** to reuse it next time.
7. **Payment** is **Cash on Delivery** — the only method. No card details are ever asked for or collected.
8. Check the **Order summary**, then click **Place order**.

**Result:** A confirmation page shows your **order number** and the amount you will pay in cash on arrival. Keep the order number — you need it to track the order.

You can check out as a guest. You do not need an account.

### Track an order

**Where:** **Track your order** at the top of any page

1. Click **Track your order** (or **Order Tracking** in the footer).
2. Enter your **Order number** and the **email address** the order was placed with.
3. Click **Track order**.

**Result:** The order's current stage is shown, along with its history. Orders move through: **Order Received → Processing → Shipping → Out for Delivery → Delivered**. An order that has been cancelled, returned or refunded says so instead.

Signed-in customers don't need this page — every order is listed under My Account.

### View your orders

**Where:** My Account → **History Orders**

1. Click the **person icon** → **My Account**.
2. Choose **History Orders** from the account menu.
3. Filter the list with the tabs: **all**, **processing**, **shipping**, **delivered**, **cancelled**.
4. Click an order to open **Order Details** — its items, prices, delivery address and the full status history.

**Result:** A complete view of everything you have ordered.

### Cancel an order

**Where:** My Account → History Orders → the order → **Cancel Order**

1. Open the order from **History Orders**.
2. If the order is still at an early stage, a **Cancel Order** button is shown.
3. Click it and confirm.

**Result:** The order is cancelled and no longer goes out for delivery.

You can only cancel while the order is still being processed. Once it has been confirmed for delivery the button no longer appears — contact Customer Service instead, who can cancel it for you.

### Return something after delivery

Returns are arranged by **Customer Service**, not from your account.

1. Contact the store (use the **CONTACT** page or the phone number in the footer) with your order number and what you want to return.
2. Customer Service records the return for you and tells you the return-shipping fee, if any. Nothing is deducted until you agree to it.
3. The items are collected and checked, and the refund is issued afterwards.

Only a delivered order can be returned.

### Refuse a delivery

You can refuse the whole order or part of it at the door, and pay only for what you keep. Nothing needs to be done in the system beforehand.

### Write a product review

**Where:** Product page → **Reviews** tab

1. Sign in.
2. Open the product and click the **Reviews** tab.
3. Choose a star rating, and optionally add a **Title** and your review text.
4. Click **Submit review**.

**Result:** Your review is submitted. Reviews are published only after the store approves them, so it will not appear on the product page immediately. You can see everything you have submitted, and its state, under **My Account → My Reviews**. You can review each product once.

### Manage your delivery addresses

**Where:** My Account → **My Address**

1. Open **My Account → My Address**.
2. To add one: fill in the recipient name, phone, the Governorate → City → District → Area selection and the street address, then save.
3. To change one: open it, edit the fields and save.
4. To remove one: use the remove control on that address and confirm.

**Result:** Saved addresses are offered at checkout so you don't retype them.

### Change your details or password

**Where:** My Account → **Setting**

1. Open **My Account → Setting**.
2. Under **Information**, update your **Full name**, **Email address** or **Phone number** and save.
3. Under **Change Password**, enter your **Current password**, then the **New password** twice, and save.

**Result:** Your account details are updated.

### Other account pages

- **Dashboard** — a summary: total orders, orders in progress, cancelled orders, and your most recent orders.
- **Notifications** — messages about your orders. **Mark all as read** clears the unread count.
- **Recently Viewed** — products you have opened recently.
- **Wishlist** — everything you have saved.

### Contact the store

**Where:** **CONTACT** in the main menu

1. Open **CONTACT**.
2. Enter your **Name**, **Email**, an **Order number** (optional) and your **Message**.
3. Click **Send message**.

**Result:** Your message reaches the Customer Service team. Opening hours and the store's phone and email are shown on the same page.

### Frequently asked questions

**Where:** **FAQs** in the footer

Answers covering ordering, guest checkout, payment, how shipping is calculated, how to follow an order, changing or cancelling an order, refusing a delivery, and returns.

---

## 1.4 What Customers Cannot Do

- **Pay by card.** Cash on delivery is the only payment method. No card or bank details are ever collected.
- **Change a shipping price.** Shipping is set by the store for each area and is never editable.
- **Start a return online.** Returns are recorded by Customer Service on your behalf.
- **Cancel a confirmed order online.** Contact Customer Service once the order has been confirmed for delivery.

# Part 2 — The Staff Workspace

Everything below applies to every staff role. What each role can actually reach is covered in that role's own guide.

## 2.1 Getting Started

### Signing in

**Where:** the store address followed by `/admin`

1. Open the workspace address in your browser.
2. The **Sign in** page asks for your **Email** and **Password** — the ones your administrator gave you.
3. Tick **Remember me** if you want to stay signed in on this computer.
4. Click **Sign in**.

**Result:** You land on the **Dashboard**.

If the sign-in is refused, check the address and password with your administrator. Staff accounts can also be deactivated, in which case sign-in is refused until the account is reactivated.

### What you see after signing in

The screen has four parts:

- **The sidebar**, down the side: every area you have access to, grouped by subject.
- **The top bar**: the menu toggle, the WAQAR Admin title, a light/dark switch, the language switch, and your name.
- **The page title and breadcrumb trail**, telling you where you are and letting you step back.
- **The page itself.**

### Choosing your language

Click the **language switch** in the top bar and choose **العربية** or **English**. The whole workspace switches, including the text direction.

### Light or dark

Click the **sun/moon icon** in the top bar to switch between light and dark appearance. Your choice is remembered.

### Making the sidebar smaller

Click the **chevron** at the top of the sidebar to collapse it to icons only, or the **menu icon** in the top bar to hide it entirely. On a narrow screen the sidebar hides itself and the menu icon opens it as a panel.

### Signing out

Click **your name** in the top-right corner and choose **Log out**.

The Dashboard shows only the tiles that match your access. Depending on your role you may see:

- **Open returns**
- **Active products**
- **Total customers**
- **Treasury balance**
- **Latest orders** — the most recent orders, with a **View all** link
- **Low stock** — the product variants closest to running out

Tiles you have no access to are simply not shown. The Dashboard is read-only: nothing on it changes anything.

## 2.3 Patterns You Will See Everywhere

### Searching a list

Lists that support it carry a search box above the table. What it searches is written in the box itself — for example **Search order no., phone or name** on the order list, or **Search name or SKU…** on products. Type and the list narrows. Clear the box to see everything again.

### Filtering a list

Filters sit next to the search box as drop-downs — for example **All statuses** on the order list, or **All warehouses** on Inventory. Choose a value to narrow the list, and choose the "All …" option again to clear it.

### Paging

Long lists are split into pages, with page controls underneath the table.

### Exporting to Excel

Some lists carry an **Export to Excel** button above the table. Clicking it downloads what the list currently shows — including whatever search and filters you have applied — as a spreadsheet file.

Export is a separate permission from viewing. Seeing a list does not automatically mean you can export it, so the button appears only for roles that have been given it.

### Saving

Forms have a clear save button at the bottom — **Save Customer**, **Save Product**, **Create Order**, and so on. If something is missing or invalid, the field is marked in red with a message under it and nothing is saved until you fix it.

### Confirmations

Anything that removes or finalises something asks first — for example **Delete "…"?** or **Confirm Delivered?**. Nothing happens until you confirm.

### Messages

After an action, a short message appears in the corner confirming what happened, or explaining why it could not be done.

### Why your menu may look different from a colleague's

The workspace shows each person only what their role allows. Two people looking at the same screen may also see **different rows**: Customer Service agents see only the orders they placed themselves, their team leader sees the whole team's, and the Store Orders role sees only orders that came from the website.

# Part 3 — How an Order Moves Through the Business

One order passes through several teams. Each team works from its own screen, and each step is described in full in that role's guide.

1. **The order is placed** — either by the customer on the storefront, or by **Customer Service** using **Create Order**. Stock is reserved at this point, but not yet deducted.
2. **Checking reviews it** — the **Checking** team opens the order from the **Checking** queue and either **Confirms**, **Postpones**, marks it **Backorder**, or **Cancels** it.
3. **Delivery assigns it** — the **Delivery Manager** opens the **Delivery Board** and assigns each confirmed order to one of the store's own representatives or to a shipping company.
4. **Accounting records the result** — once the order is out, the **Accounting** team opens it from the **Accounting** screen and records what actually happened: **Delivered**, **Returned**, or **Partially Returned**, together with the money collected and which treasury account it went into. **Stock is only deducted at this point.**
5. **If the customer returns something afterwards** — **Customer Service** files the return, the customer's agreement to any return-shipping fee is recorded, the **Warehouse Manager** approves it and receives the items back into stock, and the refund is recorded against a treasury account.
6. **Money is settled** — for orders handed to a shipping company, **Accounting** produces a statement for a period on the **Reconciliation** screen and records the transfer when the company pays.

# Part 5 — Not Available in This Release

These are listed so nobody spends time looking for them. Each is handled another way, or not at all, today.

| Not available | What to do instead |
|---|---|
| **A reports section** | The Dashboard tiles, and **Export to Excel** on the orders, checking, returns, inventory and product lists. |
| **A screen to create or edit discount coupons** | Coupon codes already set up can be applied at checkout and on **Create Order**, but there is no screen to add or change one. |
| **Approving or rejecting product reviews** | Customers can submit reviews, and see their own under My Reviews, but there is no screen to approve them, so submitted reviews stay unpublished. |
| **A screen to add or edit warehouses** | The existing warehouses are selectable wherever a warehouse is needed. |
| **Moving stock between warehouses** | Use **Inventory → Adjust** on each warehouse separately, with a reason recorded. |
| **A dedicated expenses screen** | Record an expense on **Treasury → Record Transaction**, choosing **Expense**. |
| **Customers starting a return themselves** | Customer Service files the return on the customer's behalf. |

The **Roles & Permissions** screen lists a few permissions — such as viewing reports, managing coupons, moderating reviews, managing warehouses, transferring stock and managing expenses — that do not yet have a screen behind them. Granting one of these has no visible effect today.
